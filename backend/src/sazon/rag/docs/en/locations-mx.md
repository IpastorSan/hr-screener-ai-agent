# Branches and delivery areas in Mexico

Grupo Sazón has 25 branches with their own delivery team in Mexico. Each driver always works from the same branch and delivers within its area, usually within a radius of about 4 km. The branch is assigned by the neighborhood where you live, so the trip from home to the branch is short.

## Opening hours

Every branch delivers every day of the week, with a lunch shift from 13:00 to 17:00 and a dinner shift from 19:00 to 23:00. In-person hiring interviews take place at the branch itself with its manager, Monday to Friday, between 10:00 and 14:00 or between 16:00 and 20:00.
