# Pay and payments in Mexico

This document summarises how delivery work is paid at Grupo Sazón in Mexico. Amounts are in Mexican pesos (MXN), before income tax (ISR) withholding and IMSS contributions.

## Weekly pay by city

Pay ranges from 2,100 to 2,600 pesos (MXN) a week for full time, depending on the branch's city:

- Mexico City, Naucalpan, Monterrey, San Pedro Garza García, Guadalupe, Tijuana and Cancún: 2,600 MXN a week.
- Guadalajara, Zapopan, Tlaquepaque, Querétaro, Puebla, Toluca and Hermosillo: 2,350 MXN a week.
- Mérida, León, Aguascalientes and San Luis Potosí: 2,100 MXN a week.

Full time is 48 hours a week, in six shifts, with one rest day. People who work fewer hours get the proportional share of their city's weekly pay.

## When and how you get paid

Pay is weekly, on Fridays, by direct deposit into a bank account in the worker's name. Each payment comes with its official payslip (CFDI) showing the days worked, the per-kilometre allowance if you use your own motorbike, card tips and withholdings.

## Overtime, Sundays and statutory holidays

Overtime is voluntary and is paid as the Federal Labour Law requires: the first nine overtime hours of the week are paid double and any further hours triple.

Anyone working on a Sunday gets a Sunday premium of 25 % of that day's wage. Statutory rest days that are worked, such as 1 January or 16 September, are paid triple.

The dinner shift ends at 23:00, so there is no full night shift; dinner hours are paid the same as lunch hours.

## Per-kilometre allowance for your own motorbike

If you deliver with your own motorbike you get 2.50 MXN per kilometre driven on deliveries, calculated by the app from each order's route. It covers fuel, wear and maintenance and is deposited with the weekly pay. There is no allowance with a company motorbike, because Grupo Sazón pays its fuel and maintenance.

## Tips

Drivers keep all their tips, on top of their pay. Card tips are added to the week's payment; cash tips are kept by the driver the same day.

## Statutory benefits

Besides the wage, the payroll contract includes the statutory benefits. The aguinaldo, the Christmas bonus, is paid before 20 December and equals 15 days' wages. There are also paid holidays from the first year with a 25 % holiday bonus, profit sharing (PTU) when it applies, and registration with the IMSS and Infonavit.

## Bonuses

Grupo Sazón pays a 1,500 MXN bonus for referring a friend or relative who joins the delivery team and is still working there after three months. There are no bonuses for the number of orders or for speed: pay is the same whether there are many orders or few.

The first training shift is paid like any other shift.
