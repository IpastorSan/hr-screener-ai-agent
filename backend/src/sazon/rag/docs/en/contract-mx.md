# Contract and hours in Mexico

In Mexico Grupo Sazón drivers work under a payroll contract signed directly with Grupo Sazón México, S.A. de C.V. It is not a fee-based (honorarios) contract, nor through an outsourcing company or a platform.

## Type of contract

The contract is a payroll contract of indefinite duration, with all the statutory benefits set by the Federal Labour Law. You can work full time or fewer hours depending on the availability you give, and pay and benefits are calculated in proportion to the hours.

## IMSS registration and statutory benefits

You are registered with the IMSS from day one, including the training shift. Registration gives you medical care at IMSS clinics, cover for work-related risks, paid sick leave, retirement savings (Afore) and access to Infonavit housing credit.

The statutory benefits include:

- The aguinaldo, 15 days' wages, paid before 20 December.
- Paid holidays: 12 days in the first year, increasing with seniority.
- A holiday bonus (prima vacacional) of 25 % of the holiday days.
- Profit sharing (PTU) when it applies.

Registration needs your social security number (NSS); if you do not have it, you can look it up or request it online on the IMSS portal with your CURP.

## Probation period

The contract has a 30-day probation period, as the Federal Labour Law allows. During that period the days worked are paid in full, with their tips and per-kilometre allowance, and statutory benefits accrue from day one.

## Hours and schedule

Full time is 48 hours a week over six shifts, with one weekly rest day. There are also contracts from 12 hours a week. The schedule is published every week based on the availability given, with lunch and dinner shifts, and most schedules include at least one weekend day. Anyone who rests on a weekday and works on Sunday gets the Sunday premium.

The weekly rest day usually falls between Monday and Wednesday, the days with the fewest orders.

If your availability changes, tell the branch manager a week in advance so the schedule can be adjusted. You can ask for more contract hours at any time; they are granted when the branch has free shifts.

## Statutory rest days

The statutory rest days, such as 1 January, 5 February, 1 May, 16 September or 25 December, are paid even when not worked. Anyone working on one of those days is paid triple for it.

## Ending the employment

If you want to leave, tell the manager at least one week in advance. When you leave you return the uniform, the waterproof jacket and the insulated backpack, and you receive your final settlement (finiquito) with the proportional aguinaldo, holidays and holiday bonus.
