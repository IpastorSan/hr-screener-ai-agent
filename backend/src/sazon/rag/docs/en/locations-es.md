# Kitchens and delivery areas in Spain

Grupo Sazón has 20 kitchens with their own delivery team in Spain. Each rider always works from the same kitchen and delivers within its area, usually within a radius of about 4 km. The kitchen is assigned by where you live, so the trip from home to the kitchen is short.

## Opening hours

Every kitchen delivers every day of the week, with a lunch shift from 12:30 to 16:30 and a dinner shift from 19:30 to 23:30. In-person hiring interviews take place at the kitchen itself with its manager, Monday to Friday, between 10:00 and 14:00 or between 16:00 and 20:00.
