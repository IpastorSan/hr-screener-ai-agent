# Motorbikes, license and insurance in Mexico

This document explains what vehicle Grupo Sazón drivers use in Mexico, which license is needed and which insurance covers the driver and the motorbike.

## Company motorbikes

You do not need your own motorbike: most branches have company motorbikes for the shifts. They are 125 to 150 cc motorcycles. You collect the motorbike at the branch at the start of the shift and return it at the end. The company motorbike cannot be taken home or used outside the shift.

With a company motorbike, fuel, maintenance, emissions testing (verificación) and plate paperwork are paid by Grupo Sazón. Each motorbike has its own fuel card, used only for that motorbike. Breakdowns and dents are reported to the manager the same day.

## Driver's license

You need a valid license that covers motorcycles in your state, such as a type A license in Mexico City. In Jalisco, Nuevo León and most other states it is the motorcyclist license. A car-only license does not work unless it includes motorcycles. If you are still getting your license, you can apply again once you have it.

## Your own motorbike

If you prefer to use your own motorbike you get 2.50 MXN per kilometre driven on deliveries. In Mexico your own motorbike must have valid plates and circulation card, an up-to-date emissions test where it applies, and at least liability insurance. Before your first shift the branch checks the motorbike's papers: circulation card, insurance policy and, where it applies, the emissions test.

Fuel, maintenance and repairs of your own motorbike are the owner's responsibility, and the per-kilometre allowance is meant to cover them.

## Insurance on company motorbikes

Company motorbikes have full-coverage insurance, taken out and paid by Grupo Sazón. It covers third-party damage, damage to the motorbike itself, total theft, the riders' medical expenses and roadside assistance. The driver never pays the deductible or the damage from a crash with a company motorbike, unless they caused it on purpose.

## Insurance on your own motorbike

Your own motorbike must carry at least liability insurance, which covers third-party damage. Damage to your own motorbike in an accident is only covered if your policy includes it; Grupo Sazón does not cover material damage to drivers' own motorbikes.

## If you have an accident

In Mexico you are registered with the IMSS from day one, which covers work-related risks both during the shift and on the way between home and the branch. The IMSS provides medical care free of charge and pays 100 % of your wage during sick leave for a work-related accident.

If there is an accident: get to safety, call 911 if anyone is hurt, tell the manager and call the insurer's number on the motorbike's card; do not settle anything without the insurance adjuster. The manager helps you report it to the IMSS.

## Helmet and protective gear

Company motorbikes come with a certified helmet and gloves. With your own motorbike you can use your own helmet if it is certified.
