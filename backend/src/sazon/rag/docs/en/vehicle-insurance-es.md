# Motorbikes, licence and insurance in Spain

This document explains what vehicle Grupo Sazón drivers use in Spain, which licence is needed and which insurance covers the driver and the motorbike.

## Company motorbikes

You do not need your own motorbike: most kitchens have company motorbikes for the shifts. They are 125 cc scooters and, at some kitchens, 49 cc mopeds. You collect the motorbike at the kitchen at the start of the shift and return it at the end. The company motorbike cannot be taken home or used outside the shift.

With a company motorbike, fuel, maintenance, servicing and the ITV roadworthiness test are paid by Grupo Sazón. Each motorbike has its own fuel card, used only for that motorbike. Breakdowns and dents are reported to the shift manager the same day.

## Driving licence

For the 125 cc company motorbikes an A1, A2 or A licence is valid, or a B licence held for more than 3 years; for the 49 cc ones an AM licence is enough. The licence must be valid. If you are still getting your licence, you can apply again once you have it.

With your own motorbike, your licence must cover that motorbike's engine size.

## Your own motorbike

If you prefer to use your own motorbike you get an allowance of €0.15 per kilometre driven on deliveries. In Spain your own motorbike must have a valid ITV and up-to-date compulsory insurance; anything from 49 cc works. Before your first shift the kitchen checks the motorbike's papers: registration certificate, ITV and insurance receipt.

Fuel, maintenance and repairs of your own motorbike are the owner's responsibility, and the per-kilometre allowance is meant to cover them.

## Insurance on company motorbikes

Company motorbikes have comprehensive insurance, taken out and paid by Grupo Sazón. It covers third-party damage, damage to the motorbike itself, theft and roadside assistance. The driver never pays the excess or the damage from a crash with a company motorbike, unless they caused it on purpose.

## Insurance on your own motorbike

Your own motorbike must carry its own compulsory insurance, which covers third-party damage. Damage to your own motorbike in an accident is only covered if your policy includes it; Grupo Sazón does not cover material damage to drivers' own motorbikes.

## If you have an accident

In Spain you are registered with Social Security from day one, which covers work accidents both during the shift and on the way between home and the kitchen. Medical care is provided free by Grupo Sazón's mutual insurance partner (mutua), and sick leave after a work accident is paid from the day after the accident.

If there is an accident: get to safety, call 112 if anyone is hurt, tell the shift manager and fill in the joint accident report (parte amistoso) with the other party; there is one in the top box of every company motorbike. The shift manager handles the insurer and the mutua.

## Helmet and protective gear

Company motorbikes come with an approved helmet and gloves. With your own motorbike you can use your own helmet if it is approved.
