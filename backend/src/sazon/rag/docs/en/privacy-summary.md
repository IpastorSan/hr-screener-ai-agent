# Privacy notice summary for applicants

This is a summary of Grupo Sazón's privacy notice for applicants. The full notice is on the privacy page of the jobs site. It complies with the GDPR and the LOPDGDD in Spain, and with the Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties (LFPDPPP) in Mexico.

## Who is responsible for the data

In Spain the controller is Grupo Sazón Restauración, S.L., at calle del Pimentón 14, 28012 Madrid. In Mexico it is Grupo Sazón México, S.A. de C.V., in colonia Roma Norte, alcaldía Cuauhtémoc, 06700 Mexico City. In both countries, any privacy question goes to privacidad@gruposazon.example, which is also the contact for the data protection officer.

## What data is used and why

We use your full name, mobile phone number and email; your screening answers, such as your driving licence, area, availability, preferred schedule, delivery experience and start date; and your conversation with Ana and your interview appointment. The data is only used for this Grupo Sazón hiring process, to keep them in our talent pool for future vacancies if you agree in the chat, and to improve the chat and produce aggregate statistics. We do not ask for health, religion or ethnic data, we do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

## AI assistant

The chat is run by Ana, an artificial intelligence assistant, not a person. The chat only starts if you accept the notice; if you do not, it ends and nothing is stored. Ana closes an application on her own in these cases only: not having a valid driving licence, living outside every delivery area, essential data still missing after several tries (first name and surname, the licence, the city, or a phone number or email to make contact; the applicant can start again), or an attempt to manipulate the assistant. The applicant can also stop at any time. If she closes an application, a person from the recruiting team can be asked to review it. Hiring decisions are always made by Grupo Sazón's recruiting team.

## Who else has access to the data

The AI models Ana uses are accessed through OpenRouter with zero data retention: messages are not kept after the reply and are not used to train models. The servers and the database are on Google Cloud, in the Madrid region (European Union), also for applications from Mexico. We use no third-party analytics tools or advertising cookies.

## How long the data is kept

The application and the conversation are kept for at most 12 months from the last message and are then deleted. Data in the talent pool is also kept for at most 12 months from the last message, or until you ask us to delete it. If you are hired, your data moves to your employee file, which has its own employee privacy notice.

## How to exercise your rights

You can ask for your data to be deleted at any time by writing to privacidad@gruposazon.example, giving the email or phone number you used in the chat. In Spain you can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability, with a reply within one month and the option to complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD). In Mexico you can exercise your ARCO rights, with a reply within 20 working days at most.
