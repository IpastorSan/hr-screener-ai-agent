# Hiring process in Spain

This document explains what happens between the end of your chat with Ana and your first delivery shift at a Grupo Sazón kitchen in Spain.

## After the chat

Ana is Grupo Sazón's recruiting assistant and she is an AI. When the chat ends, if your profile fits, an interview, at the kitchen or by video call, is offered right in the chat; if it does not, the reason is explained in the same chat. Ana closes an application on her own in these cases only: not having a valid driving licence, living outside every delivery area, essential data still missing after several tries (first name and surname, the licence, the city, or a phone number or email to make contact; the applicant can start again), or an attempt to manipulate the assistant. The applicant can also stop at any time. Hiring decisions are always made by Grupo Sazón's recruiting team, after the interview.

## The interview

The interview is short, about 15 minutes, and takes place in person at the kitchen assigned to you, with the manager of the location. Interviews run Monday to Friday, in the morning between 10:00 and 14:00 or in the afternoon between 16:00 and 20:00. If you prefer, it can be a video call with the recruiting team. You pick the time in the chat from the free slots, and if you need to change it you can come back to the chat and pick another.

The kitchen's shift manager meets you. They talk about your availability, the areas you know and your experience, show you the kitchen and the motorbikes, and answer your questions about the schedule and pay. There is no riding test and no written test.

## What to bring to the interview

Bring your DNI or, if you are a foreign national, your NIE or residence card with a work permit, and your valid driving licence. If you will deliver with your own motorbike, also bring its registration certificate, ITV and insurance receipt. You do not need a CV.

## Documents for the contract

To sign the contract you need your DNI or NIE with a work permit, your Social Security number, your driving licence and a bank account for your pay. If you do not have a Social Security number yet, you can request it from the Social Security Treasury before signing. Documents are handed in at the kitchen or uploaded to the delivery app as photos.

## Training

Before delivering on your own there is a single paid training shift alongside someone from the team. In that shift you learn to use the app, check the motorbike and find your way around the kitchen's area, and you make your first deliveries with your partner.

## When you start

It usually takes one to two weeks from the interview to the first shift, depending on the paperwork and the kitchen's openings. You hear the result of the interview within three working days at most. If you are hired, you sign the contract and receive your first week's schedule in the app. If there is no opening at the time, you can ask to be told when there is one.
