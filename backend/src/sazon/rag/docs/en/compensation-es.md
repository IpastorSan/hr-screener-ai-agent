# Pay and payments in Spain

This document summarises how delivery work is paid at Grupo Sazón in Spain. All amounts are gross, before income tax withholding (IRPF) and Social Security contributions.

## Hourly pay by city

Pay ranges from €9.50 to €11 gross per hour depending on the city and the shift. The base hourly rate depends on the kitchen's city:

- Madrid, Barcelona, L'Hospitalet de Llobregat and Palma: €10.25 gross per hour.
- Valencia, Seville, Málaga, Bilbao, Zaragoza and Las Palmas de Gran Canaria: €9.75 gross per hour.
- Alicante, Murcia, Valladolid and Granada: €9.50 gross per hour.

The hourly rate already includes the prorated share of the two extra payments, so there are no separate extra payments in June and December.

## When and how you get paid

You are paid monthly in arrears by bank transfer, before the 5th of the following month. The payslip arrives by email with the hours worked, the mileage allowance if you use your own motorbike, card tips and withholdings.

To get paid you need a bank account in your name, which is requested when you sign the contract.

## Nights, overtime and public holidays

Hours worked from 22:00 onwards carry a night allowance of €0.75 gross per hour on top of the base rate. That is why the dinner shift in Madrid or Barcelona reaches €11 gross per hour from that time.

On part-time contracts, additional hours you accept outside the published schedule are paid at the same rate as the shift hour. On full-time contracts overtime is voluntary and is also paid at the shift rate or taken as time off, as the driver chooses.

Weekends are paid the same as weekdays. Public holidays worked are compensated with an extra day off.

## Mileage allowance for your own motorbike

If you deliver with your own motorbike you get an allowance of €0.15 per kilometre driven on deliveries, calculated by the app from each order's route. It covers fuel, wear and maintenance and is paid on the same payslip. There is no allowance with a company motorbike, because Grupo Sazón pays its fuel and maintenance.

## Tips

Drivers keep all their tips, on top of their pay. Card tips are added to the month's payslip; cash tips are kept by the driver the same day.

## Bonuses

Grupo Sazón pays a €100 gross bonus for referring a friend or relative who joins the delivery team and is still working there after three months. There are no bonuses for the number of orders or for delivery speed: pay is per hour worked, whether there are many orders or few.

## Paid training

The first training shift is paid like any other shift, at your city's hourly rate.
