# Contract and hours in Spain

In Spain Grupo Sazón drivers work under an employment contract signed directly with Grupo Sazón Restauración, S.L. Nobody works as a freelancer (autónomo) or through a temp agency.

## Type of contract

The contract is permanent (indefinido), part time or full time depending on the availability each person gives. The so-called rider law (Ley 12/2021) establishes that people who deliver for a company are employees; Grupo Sazón has never used bogus freelancers and its whole delivery team is on staff.

The hospitality collective agreement of each kitchen's province applies, setting holidays, leave and minimum pay scales. Grupo Sazón pays above those scales.

## Social Security registration

You are registered with Social Security from your first day of work, including the training shift. Registration means contributions to your pension, unemployment benefit and sick pay, and covers work accidents and accidents on the way between home and the kitchen. To register you need your Social Security number; if you do not have one yet, you can request it from the Social Security Treasury before signing.

## Probation period

The contract has a one-month probation period. During that month either side can end the employment without notice; the days worked are paid in full, as are tips and mileage.

## Hours and schedule

Contracts start at 12 hours a week and go up to full time at 40 hours. The schedule is published every week based on the availability given, with lunch and dinner shifts, and most schedules include at least one weekend day. There are always at least 12 hours of rest between the end of one shift and the start of the next, and at least a day and a half off in a row each week.

If your availability changes, for example because of studies, tell the shift manager a week in advance so the schedule can be adjusted. You can ask for more contract hours at any time; they are granted when the kitchen has free shifts.

## Holidays and leave

Holidays are 30 calendar days a year, or the proportional share of the time worked. They are requested a month in advance and spread out so the kitchen is never short of drivers on the busiest dates. Paid leave, such as for a wedding, a birth or a house move, follows the collective agreement.

## Ending the contract

If you want to leave after the probation period, give 15 days' notice. When you leave you return the uniform, the waterproof jacket and the insulated backpack, and you receive your final settlement (finiquito) including any untaken holidays.
