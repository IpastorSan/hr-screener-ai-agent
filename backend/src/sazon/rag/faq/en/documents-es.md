also: What paperwork is needed for the contract? | What should I bring to the interview? | Do I need a DNI or NIE? | Do I need a work permit?
---
For the interview your DNI or NIE and your driving licence are enough. For the contract you need your DNI or NIE with a work permit, your Social Security number, your driving licence and a bank account for your pay.
